# Northwind Instruments Third Quarter Fiscal 2025 Results

> Fictional document created for evaluation purposes. Northwind Instruments does not exist.

Northwind Instruments reported third quarter fiscal 2025 revenue of $412.3 million, an increase of 11% compared with the third quarter of fiscal 2024. Gross margin for the quarter was 58.2%, compared with 56.9% in the prior-year quarter. Operating income was $71.5 million, or 17.3% of revenue. Net income was $54.8 million, or $1.37 per diluted share.

The Sensors segment contributed $236.0 million of revenue, while the Analytics Software segment contributed $176.3 million. Cash and cash equivalents totaled $689.4 million at the end of the quarter, and the company had no outstanding borrowings under its credit facility.

The board of directors authorized a share repurchase program of up to $150 million over the next 24 months. Management raised full-year fiscal 2025 revenue guidance to a range of $1.62 billion to $1.66 billion, up from the prior range of $1.58 billion to $1.62 billion.

Chief Executive Officer Maria Okafor said that demand for industrial vibration sensors remained strong in Europe and Asia. Chief Financial Officer Daniel Reyes noted that the company expects capital expenditures of approximately $48 million for the full year.
