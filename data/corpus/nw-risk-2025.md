# Northwind Instruments Annual Report Excerpt: Business and Risk Factors

> Fictional document created for evaluation purposes. Northwind Instruments does not exist.

Northwind's headquarters is located in Austin, Texas. The company had 1,240 employees as of the end of fiscal 2024.

Northwind relies on a single contract manufacturer in Penang, Malaysia, for approximately 62% of its sensor production. A disruption at this facility could delay shipments and reduce revenue. In fiscal 2024, the company's largest customer accounted for 14% of total revenue.

Export controls on certain semiconductor components could increase costs or restrict sales to some customers. Northwind has never paid a cash dividend and does not currently intend to do so.
