# Harborline Logistics Second Quarter 2025 Earnings Call

> Fictional document created for evaluation purposes. Harborline Logistics does not exist.

Harborline Logistics reported second quarter 2025 revenue of $1.08 billion, up 6% year over year. Adjusted EBITDA was $162 million, representing a margin of 15.0%. Harborline handled 3.4 million shipments in the second quarter of 2025, compared with 3.1 million in the second quarter of 2024.

Chief Executive Officer Priya Nair said fuel surcharges reduced margins by approximately 40 basis points. The company opened two new distribution centers in Rotterdam and Singapore during the quarter.

Harborline lowered its full-year adjusted EBITDA margin outlook to between 14.5% and 15.0%, from the prior range of 15.0% to 15.5%. Management reaffirmed full-year revenue guidance of $4.3 billion to $4.4 billion. Chief Financial Officer Tomas Lindqvist said net debt stood at $1.9 billion, or 2.9 times trailing adjusted EBITDA.
